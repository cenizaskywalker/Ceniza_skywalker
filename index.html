03 / ABOUT

Design with

I'm Ceniza, a Roblox UI Designer focused on creating polished, functional, and visually distinctive interfaces.

I enjoy turning ideas into UI systems that feel natural inside a game — from a single button or HUD element to complete menus, inventories, shops, and progression systems.

My main workflow is built around Figma, with a focus on visual consistency, usability, responsive layouts, and attention to the small details that make an interface feel finished.
